NHOM4 - CHAY NHANH

A. CHAY EXPO GO (AI GEMINI)
1. Cai Node.js 22.13+ va Expo Go. Dien thoai + laptop cung Wi-Fi.
2. Chay RUN_WINDOWS.bat. Lan dau file tao server/.env va mo Notepad.
3. Tao Gemini API key MOI (thu hoi 2 key da lo trong chat), dan vao GEMINI_API_KEY trong server/.env, Save.
4. Chay lai RUN_WINDOWS.bat, cho cai thu vien va QR.
5. Quet QR bang Expo Go. Cho phep Camera/Photos/Location khi app hoi.
6. Bat cong tac Gemini neu muon gui anh len may chu.
7. Kiem tra http://localhost:3000/api/ai/health phai tra ok:true, NHOM4_AI_OK.

B. CHAY AI OFFLINE TFLITE
1. Expo Go KHONG chay TFLite native.
2. Chay npx eas-cli@latest login va npx eas-cli@latest init (lan dau).
3. Tao Google Maps Android SDK key, bat Maps SDK for Android; gioi han package com.nhom4.smartwaste va SHA-1. Khai bao GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY cho EAS hoac root .env.
4. Chay BUILD_ANDROID_OFFLINE.bat, tai APK Development Build va cai len dien thoai.
5. Chay RUN_DEV_CLIENT_WINDOWS.bat tren laptop, mo app Development Build tren dien thoai, ket noi Metro.
6. Lan dau model TFLite tu tai khoang 34.9 MB (can Internet); sau do duoc cache va co the nhan dien offline.

C. DATABASE (TUY CHON)
- SQL chua can de mo app, camera, Gemini, ban do.
- Muon dang ky/login, luu lich su, Raspberry Pi: chay sql/SmartTrashDB.sql trong SSMS va dien DB_* trong server/.env.

D. BAN DO
- Mo tab Ban do, cap quyen vi tri. Giu tren ban do de chon vi tri; danh sach tu tai lai quanh diem do.
- Chon marker/dong dia diem de hien tuyen di bo ngay trong app. Du lieu tu OpenStreetMap, co the thieu diem neu khu vuc chua co du lieu.

Luu y: AI phan loai chi nhan dien loai rac + nhom + cach xu ly. Chat AI la chuc nang rieng, chi gui cau hoi va ngu canh ket qua (khong gui lai anh, khong phan loai anh). Ket qua Raspberry Pi hien anh + cach xu ly + o chat; tab Tro ly AI la chat doc lap. Khong dua API key len GitHub.
